<objective>
Close CR-07's final, end-to-end half: make the retry/park/notification pipeline reachable in production. After 43-06 and 43-07 wired `record_failure` into the background upload failure paths, entries actually transition to `Failed` on disk. This plan replaces the inert `WriteQueue::default()` in `SyncDaemon` with the real `cb-journal` WriteQueue and has the sync cycle read the journal and emit `SyncStatus::WriteParked`, which the existing `sync/mod.rs` bridge (built in 43-04) turns into the tray status + OS notification.

This plan owns `crates/sdk/src/sync.rs`, `apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/sync/mod.rs`, and `apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/commands/sync.rs` — disjoint from all other gap plans. It runs in wave 6 because it depends on the `record_failure` callers added by 43-06/43-07 and on 43-05 removing the `Default` impl.

Purpose: turn the verified-but-dead WriteParked pipeline into a live park-and-notify loop.
Output: a SyncDaemon backed by the real journal that surfaces parked writes to the user.
</objective>
