<objective>
Enforce the strictly-later-EOL invariant on `renewIpnsRecord` and strengthen its tests (SC3 item 4; closes todo:2026-07-01-renew-ipns-record-eol-invariant-and-tests). Today the primitive accepts a `lifetimeMs` param but never checks that the renewed record's end-of-life is actually later than the existing record's — a caller passing a lifetime shorter than the elapsed-since-creation could mint an equal/earlier EOL (an IPNS rollback). CipherBox's own `ParsedIpnsRecord` wrapper does not currently surface `validity`, though the underlying `ipns` package's `unmarshalIPNSRecord()` already computes it.

This plan: (1) extends `ParsedIpnsRecord` additively with `validity: Date`, (2) adds a strictly-later-EOL guard comparing against the PARSED EXISTING record's validity (never wall-clock), plus a sanitized try/catch, and (3) upgrades the renewal test to assert the invariant directly with a longer-than-default original-lifetime edge case.

Purpose: no IPNS record renewal can regress or hold an end-of-life.
Output: additive codec field, guarded `renewIpnsRecord`, and a real invariant test.
</objective>
