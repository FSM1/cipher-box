Grounding (RESEARCH Sharp Question 6.1, confirmed live):
- Bug A (dirty-resume `:996-1020`): seeds ONE root-only `ParentTrackingState` (`parent_ipns_name: root_ipns_name`, `:999`; `pending_child_count: frontier.len()`, `:1008`), then `root_children.iter().find(|c| c.ipns_name == entry.ipns_name)` (`:1013-1016`) and `else { continue; }` (`:1016-1018`) silently DROPS any depth>=2 entry (worse than TS — it doesn't even decrement, wedging `pending_child_count` forever). `enqueue_child` is called with hard-coded `parent_ipns_name = root_ipns_name` (`:1019`).
- Bug B (normal branch): `complete_pending_child` (`:1185-1204`) returns `false` and silently skips decrement+reseal when `parent_tracking.get_mut(parent_ipns_name)` is absent (`:1193-1195`). The Rust engine has NO equivalent of folding the pre-rotation dirty frontier into the fresh/normal branch (structural gap TS closed at engine.ts:1523-1524).
- Fix shape: build/lookup a `ParentTrackingState` per DISTINCT `parent_ipns_name` in the frontier (generalize the root-only seed at `:996-1010`), route `enqueue_child`'s parent from `entry.parent_ipns_name`, and fold the pre-rotation frontier into the walk so intermediate parents exist before their dirty children are consumed. Because `complete_pending_child` already keys off `item.parent_ipns_name`, the decrement attribution self-corrects once intermediate entries exist.

Purpose: bring the Rust twin to the same depth>=2 soundness contract as the TS engine (SC#7).
Output: depth-aware Rust consumption + FakeDeps depth>=2 tests. Implements D-11 (SC#1/SC#2 Rust).
