Grounding (RESEARCH Sharp Question 7.1/7.2, confirmed live):
- The ONLY `impl RotationDeps for` today is the test-only `FakeDeps` (`engine.rs:1533`). `rotate_read_on_scope_exit` (`grant_scope.rs:269-287`) is a `log::error! + Err(...not yet live-wired...)` stub taking `_api` (unused).
- Method → primitive map: `resolve` → `cipherbox_api_client::ipns::resolve_ipns_verified` (`api-client/src/ipns.rs:169`, the verified/fail-closed chokepoint; map `VerifyError::Invalid` → RotationError, genuine not-found → `Ok(None)`); `fetch_node` → `ipfs::fetch_content` + `cipherbox_core::node::decode_published_node` (used in replay.rs:938/1073); `publish_with_cas` → `ipns::publish_ipns` (`api-client/src/ipns.rs:401`; request-build precedent replay.rs:367/617). Pitfall 7: `publish_ipns`'s `PublishResult::Conflict` returns ONLY `current_sequence_number` — on a 409 the adapter must resolve+fetch_node to materialize the `remote: PublishedNode` the engine's merge needs.
- Deps come from `CipherBoxFS` (`fs.rs:32-37`): `api: Arc<ApiClient>`, `private_key`/`public_key` (owner keypair — the D-01 unwrap/wrap keys, right there), `rt`, plus a `JsonSidecarFloorStore` handle (Plan 03). The adapter does ECIES wrap/unwrap itself (option b) and delegates persist/get/delete_wrapped_key to the combined floor store.
- Async bridge already solved: `run_scope_exit_gate` (`:308-339`) runs `fs.rt.block_on(gate_scope_exit(...))`; only the body of `rotate_read_on_scope_exit` changes. Two inputs the stub lacks: the grant-root `node_id` and `root_read_key` (the inode carries the folder read key as `InodeKind::Folder { read_key, .. }`, seen delete.rs:82-87). The current `deleted_child_id` param is the WRITE-plane key — do NOT conflate.
- ROT-04 decision (see verification): `query_grants_rooted_at`/`update_grant`/`delete_grant` MAY start as trait default no-ops.

Purpose: turn the fail-closed EIO stub into a real, sound desktop rotation (D-14), on top of the now-sound engine (D-17).
Output: the NEW adapter + live wiring + floor handle, gated by adapter unit tests over a faked transport. Implements D-14.
