<objective>
Provision the D-10 / D-02 production alert: a Grafana alert that fires when the rate of cross-user unpin attempts (`cipherbox_unpin_cross_user_attempts_total`, declared in 42-01, incremented inside `guardedUnpin` in 42-03) exceeds zero over a 5-minute window. The alert turns the silent server-side telemetry into an actionable ops notification without changing the opaque client contract (D-11).
