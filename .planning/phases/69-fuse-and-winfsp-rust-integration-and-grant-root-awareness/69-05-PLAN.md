<objective>
Port the grant-root scope-exit predicate to Rust: `has_covering_grant` + `maybe_rotate_on_scope_exit` — a 1:1 translation of the shipped, unit-tested `packages/sdk-core/src/rotation/scope.ts` (60 lines, pure, no I/O). This is the single lowest-risk logic port and the algorithmic heart of SC#3: it decides whether a delete/move/rename is a private relink (zero rotation) or a shared-scope exit (exactly one `rotate_read_from_node`). It cross-checks the relay-supplied active-grant set against the client's own `local_grant_record` so a malicious/buggy relay cannot suppress rotation (T-63-17).

Per D-02 this pure predicate lives in `crates/sdk` (mirrors `packages/sdk-core`); the 69-07 FUSE module supplies the mounted-tree ancestor walk and consumes it (Pitfall 1 — one call site, no per-platform duplication).
