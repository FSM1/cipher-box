<objective>
Close the three cryptographic revocation gaps in the rotation engine (extends `engine.rs`), the Rust twins of Phase-64's CRIT-1/HIGH-3/HIGH-4 (D-05 parity):

- **CRIT-1 (content-key rotation):** rotating a file node mints a fresh `fileKey` and sets a `contentRekeyPending` marker — applied lazily on next write, NOT an eager re-encrypt of already-published content (ADR 0002). A holder of the old `readKey`/`fileKey` cannot decrypt the NEXT published version.
- **HIGH-3 (multi-rooted grant re-mint):** after rotating each node, query `shares` rooted at that node id and re-mint `readDescriptorRef` for every non-revoked recipient — including inner grants rooted at subtree nodes; delete the revoked recipient's row.
- **HIGH-4 (add-during-rotation merge):** on every CAS-409, re-fetch the current parent node, re-decode the read-body, and re-merge concurrently-added `SealedChildRef`s before re-sealing — never a blind re-seal from a stale in-memory list.

Purpose: SC#3 rotation soundness (revocation actually revokes; concurrent adds survive).
Output: CRIT-1 + HIGH-3 + HIGH-4 closures in engine.rs.
</objective>
