<objective>
Make the durable anti-rollback floor store atomic and non-blocking under async concurrency (SC#5). Today `JsonSidecarFloorStore::put` (crates/sdk/src/floor_store.rs) is a race-prone whole-map read-modify-write that also does blocking fs I/O directly inside an async fn; concurrent puts on different node_ids can lost-update each other, and `load_map` treats a corrupt sidecar as an empty map (silent cold-start). Push the fix DOWN into the concrete store (per RESEARCH A3), mirroring the already-correct TS `idbPut` single-transaction max-preserving pattern.

Purpose: Close the Tampering-class rollback gap (a stale/lower generation slipping past a lost-update) and the corrupt-sidecar cold-start weakening of the M1 defense, keeping the TS/Rust twins behaviorally equivalent.
Output: locked + spawn_blocking + max-preserving + fail-closed Rust floor store; guarded bump_floor; TS parity note.
</objective>
