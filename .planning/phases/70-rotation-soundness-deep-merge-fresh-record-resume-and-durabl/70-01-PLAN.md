<objective>
Create the rotation-only three-way merge `mergeRotatedChildren(base, local, remote)` as an isolated pure function, with local-wins conflict policy. This is SC#1 site foundation: the generic `folder/merge.ts::mergeChildren` (remote-wins) is correct for folder-state-desync but WRONG for rotation, where a remote-wins merge discards the rotation's D-02 re-seal and breaks navigation to an already-rotated child after a concurrent add.

Purpose: Make the local-wins policy syntactically impossible to invoke from a non-rotation call site (a separate function, not a flag), closing the merge-downgrade Elevation-of-Privilege gap.
Output: `mergeRotatedChildren` + its unit test suite; barrel export.
</objective>
