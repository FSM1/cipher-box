<objective>
Add TEE-internal epoch self-derivation and the refuse-to-renew-stale guard (D-03 / TEE-06 / §6.7-1, §6.7-3). The TEE derives `currentEpoch` from its own clock — never the relay's `currentEpoch`/`previousEpoch` scalars (which are removed from the request contract in 67-06). `decryptWithFallback` is reshaped to take a single `keyEpoch` hint (sourced from `ipns_records.key_epoch`) and refuses keys older than `currentEpoch − 1`, emitting a structured `ReEnrollRequiredError` (the signal whose client consumer is deferred to Phase 68/69).

Purpose: Make epoch authority physically internal to the enclave so a compromised relay cannot force re-encryption to a wrong epoch (Elevation), and bound key age so stale keys surface a recovery signal instead of silently renewing.
Output: `getInternalCurrentEpoch()` export, reshaped `decryptWithFallback`, `ReEnrollRequiredError`, retargeted `reEncryptForEpoch` call convention, and red→green unit tests.

RESEARCH Open Question 1 resolution (baked in): use Option B — keep `keyEpoch` as the ECIES decrypt hint from `ipns_records.key_epoch`; `getInternalCurrentEpoch()` reads `EPOCH_ZERO_TIMESTAMP_MS` (env-configured, fallback returns epoch 1 when unset) and is used ONLY for the floor guard and the re-encryption target — never as the ECIES trial epoch. This avoids the undefined clock-anchor problem while satisfying D-03's internal-derivation intent.
</objective>
