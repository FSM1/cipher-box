<objective>
Implement the recycle-bin re-link half of Success Criterion 4 (design §3.10 / D-06). `addToBin` and `restoreFromBin` in `packages/sdk/src/bin/index.ts` currently throw the Phase-65 stub marker. Restore must be a **pure re-link**: re-seal the deleted node's own `readKey` under the destination parent's `readKey` (`sealChildReadKey`, role 0x02 — already exists), exactly like a move — no content re-encryption.

**OQ-1 resolution (documented decision):** `BinEntry.nodeRef` is a plaintext in-memory `Node` and carries no `readKey` (the read key lives sealed in the parent). Design §3.10 specifies "a `SealedChildRef`-shaped link sealed under the bin's own `readKey`." The `RecycleBinMetadata` blob is already ECIES-encrypted as a whole to the owner's public key (`encryptBinMetadata`), so the concrete mechanism is: add `BinEntry.nodeReadKey?: Uint8Array` — the deleted node's own 32-byte readKey, captured at `addToBin` time via `unsealChildReadKey` from the source parent. The ECIES bin-blob encryption is the "seal under the bin's own readKey." On restore, `sealChildReadKey(nodeReadKey, destParentReadKey, …)` produces the destination link. This is the most robust path (a stored `SealedChildRef` would be sealed under the *source* parent and unusable at the destination).

Also delete the legacy `originalFolderKeyEncrypted` field and its re-encrypt-on-restore path. RESEARCH confirms the field is already absent from `packages/core/src/bin/types.ts` (Phase 62) and survives only in `bin.test.ts` fixtures (lines 724, 1063) — this plan removes those fixture references and confirms the source is clean.

**Q3 / D-01 (documented residual):** when the deleted subtree contains nodes the owner independently sub-shared, `addToBin` unlinks + bins with **no** cross-principal revoke attempt and **no** new schema/marker — the owner's reconcile-on-sync re-derives dangling grants live in Phase 66/68. Do not add any marker here.

Purpose: O(1) restore with no re-encryption; eliminate dead legacy fields. Output: implemented `addToBin` / `restoreFromBin`, the `nodeReadKey` field, and un-skipped bin tests proving the re-link AEAD asymmetry.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| bin blob at rest | RecycleBinMetadata (incl. nodeReadKey) is ECIES-encrypted to the owner's public key; the server stores only ciphertext (zero-knowledge) |
| restore re-link | the restored node's readKey is re-sealed under the destination parent readKey — never exposed in plaintext to the relay |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-65-05 | Information Disclosure | BinEntry.nodeReadKey at rest | high | mitigate | nodeReadKey lives only inside the ECIES-encrypted RecycleBinMetadata blob (encryptBinMetadata to owner pubkey); never sent unencrypted |
| T-65-06 | Tampering | restore re-link | medium | mitigate | sealChildReadKey binds readKeySealed to the destination parent + child identity (AAD); a transplanted ref fails to unseal — proven by the source-vs-destination asymmetry test |
| T-65-07 | Elevation of Privilege | Q3 cross-principal sub-share (D-01) | medium | accept | Documented residual: addToBin does not revoke owner-issued grants on the binned subtree; owner reconcile-on-sync closes the window (Phase 66/68). No new marker per D-01 |
| T-65-08 | Denial of Service | caller parent readKey buffers | medium | mitigate | D-09: never zero caller-supplied readKeys; only a minted intermediate is zeroed on failure |

No package-manager installs in this plan (T-65-SC not applicable).
