<objective>
Add the two missing role-`0x04 child-writekey` seal primitives to the Phase-62 node codec. These are the dependency-zero unblock for the entire write plane: every downstream task (shared-write.ts, rotation engine writeKey wiring, the write-revocation driver) needs to seal a child's `writeKey` into the parent write-body and walk it back out.

`sealChildReadKey` / `unsealChildReadKey` (role `0x02`) already exist in `packages/core/src/node/seal.ts` (lines 187–224). `sealChildWriteKey` / `unsealChildWriteKey` (role `0x04`) do NOT exist — confirmed by RESEARCH (grep). They are a verbatim copy of the role-`0x02` pair with the role byte changed to `0x04` and the parameter names changed from `*ReadKey` to `*WriteKey`. This is the "call/extend the codec, never re-architect" discipline (D-05).

Purpose: WRITE-01 requires the write link (`WriteChildRef.writeKeySealed`) to be `AES-GCM(child.writeKey, key=parent.writeKey, role=0x04)`. Without `sealChildWriteKey`, the write chain cannot be built and the read-only/write-only key separation cannot be proven.
Output: two exported async functions, two barrel re-exports, and a KAT/round-trip test that locks the role byte at `0x04` and proves cross-role rejection.
</objective>

|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
