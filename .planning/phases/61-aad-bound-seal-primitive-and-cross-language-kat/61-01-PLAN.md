<objective>
Land the canonical AAD builder on the TypeScript side and freeze the cross-language Known-Answer Test vector. This is the FIRST deliverable and the merge gate the whole phase blocks on (C-01): the frozen AAD byte encoding plus the committed AAD-bytes KAT fixture (all four role bytes) plus the TS `#[test]` equivalent, all green.

Purpose: The frozen encoding must be committed and KAT-green before any consumer seals a Node — a retroactive encoding change would force rotating every sealed body. This plan authors the ground-truth vector (`node-aad.json`) that the Rust twin (plan 02) asserts against.

Output: `buildNodeAad`, `uuidToBytes`, the `INVALID_AAD_INPUT` error code, barrel re-exports, the frozen `node-aad.json` (`aad_vectors`), the TS KAT + validation test, and the parity-script registration.
</objective>
