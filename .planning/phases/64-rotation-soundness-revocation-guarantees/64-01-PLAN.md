<objective>
Close the two folded binding-stability bugs (D-06) that are preconditions for rotation to be testable and sound end-to-end:

1. **Node-identity / generation preservation (CRITICAL):** `updateFolderMetadataAndPublish` currently mints a fresh UUID (`?? crypto.randomUUID()`) and resets generation to 0 (`?? 0`) whenever a caller omits the fields. A fresh UUID breaks `buildNodeAad` (the parent's sealed-child AAD binds the child UUID); generation 0 corrupts the convergence witness rotation relies on. Make both fields **required** and thread the stable `id` + current `generation` through all six `client.ts` call sites.
2. **`moveItem` destination re-seal (FLAG-63-U2):** a moved child's `readKeySealed` is sealed under the SOURCE parent's readKey; dest-path navigation AEAD-fails. Re-seal the moved child's `readKey` under the DESTINATION parent's readKey before the dest publish. The node keeps its own readKey/generation (no content re-encryption).

Purpose: rotation's convergence test (`parent.SealedChildRef[N].generation == N.envelope.generation`) and the multi-level e2e tree require a stable node identity and correctly-bound child links. Implements CONTEXT D-06 (both IN items). The OUT item (`client.ts` move dest-before-source publish-ordering durability) is explicitly Phase 68 — do NOT touch publish ordering here.
Output: required identity fields, six threaded call sites, dest re-seal, and two unit tests proving identity preservation + the move re-seal AEAD round-trip.
</objective>
