          - RECOMMENDED: Use inline login approach. The InvitePage has two phases: (1) unauthenticated — show invite card + login form, (2) authenticated — auto-claim and redirect.

          - "Claiming your share..." with loading indicator

       d. **Error states** — red-bordered card:
