**Finding 1 (comment 4111567305, `join_standing`): valid, but I did not use the suggested code.**
- **Can it occur:** Yes. Each device keeps its own bookmarks in its local staging store (`cbx/rs/`). So a second device of a granted account has no bookmark, and its live link read still finds the personal grant. The old code classed this as `Absent`: the join posted a needless claim and held the link.
- **Why not the suggestion:** With it, the join returns early and writes no bookmark, so that device never shows the folder.
- **Fix:** A new standing `JoinStanding::Unbookmarked`. The join records the bookmark only, with no claim and no link hold.
- **Preview:** It offers "join", not "joined". The reason: a preview saves nothing, and "open folder" would open a folder this device has no bookmark for. This is different from your proposed test, which expected "joined".
- **Test:** `a_granted_account_on_a_device_with_no_bookmark_joins_with_no_claim` in `owner_actions.rs`. It checks that the preview is not joined, that no claim is posted, and that after a tick the device reads the share as `Granted` with via-link false. Without the fix it fails at "the join posted no claim".

**Finding 2 (comment 4111567309, `open_write_seed_on_access`): valid, fixed as suggested.**
- **Evidence:** When the scope is leased, `advance_write_epoch_on_sight` in `gate/floor.rs` returns the old floor and does not raise it. The blob then stays closed, and the miss was kept for the full consult interval, even after the lease was released.
