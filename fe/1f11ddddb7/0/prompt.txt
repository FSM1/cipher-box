Two gotchas worth flagging for a manual prod-build test against the local stack:

1. **CORS.** A packaged Tauri webview's origin is `tauri://localhost` (`apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/oauth.rs:5`), but `apps/api/.env.example` ships `CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS=http://localhost:5173` and `corsOptionsFromEnv` rejects unlisted origins (`apps/api/src/app-setup.ts:107-115`).
