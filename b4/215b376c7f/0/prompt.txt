## Key findings

- The Portal is not a problem. The capture harness renders one cell per frame, so a modal never escapes its card and never stacks with a sibling cell. No override is required for correctness.
- Card-mode overrides are still wanted for density, and are recorded in the learnings file. Every dialog sits centered in a tall frame with about 60 percent empty backdrop. `DetailsDialog.FolderDetailsCard` is the only cell that clips, at the `// TIMESTAMPS` heading.
- Media dialogs render error states, as the brief allowed. With `folderKey={null}` they show `Folder key not available`. With a real key and no seeded keypair they reach the decrypt attempt and show `No keypair available - please log in again`. That gives two distinct real states per media dialog. The grade notes say so.
- The open prop is not uniform: 12 dialogs take `open`, `ShareDialog` takes `isOpen`, and `ReplaceFileDialog` has no open prop and opens itself from a non-empty `replacements` array.
- The `.d.ts` files declare `item` and `folderKey` as non-nullable, but every source component accepts `null`. Passing `null` is what the error and empty stories need.
- `MoveDialog` and the folder branch of `DetailsDialog` read `useFolderStore`; both are seeded at module top with full `FolderNode` records (bigint `sequenceNumber`, `Uint8Array` keys).
- `PdfPreviewDialog`, `AudioPlayerDialog` and `VideoPlayerDialog` pass no `title` to `Modal`, so their header bar holds only the close button. That is real component behaviour, not a missing prop.</result>
