### Residual Risks (Deferred, User-Accepted — Non-Blocking)

**RR-01 — `rotation-concurrent-add-merge-downgrades-rotated-child-readkey`:** When a CAS-409 hits during the D-09 batched parent re-publish, `mergeChildren` remote-wins can overwrite the D-02 re-sealed child ref with the pre-D-02 version (remote was published before D-02 ran). The concurrent child is NOT dropped (T-64-05b/T-64-05c closed), but that child's `readKeySealed` is bound to the old parent readKey, breaking subsequent navigation. Security impact: **liveness only** — the merged parent body is under `readKeyPrime`, inaccessible to the revoked reader. Phase 65/66 mitigation path: prefer local-wins for known-rotated child refs during merge.

**RR-02 — `rotation-fresh-record-resume-and-sc4-double-bump`:** A cold-restart resume with empty `completedNodeIds` and the original `rootReadKey` is not viable mid-walk (root is already sealed under `readKeyPrime_root`; passing the old key to `verifySubtreeClean` causes AEAD failure). The Test 2 crash scenario is viable only at the final persist call (all D-09 publishes complete). Genuine mid-walk crash recovery requires a persisted `readKeyPrime` from the host, which is Phase-68 (IndexedDB durable floor). Security impact: **durability only** — the revoked reader is already cut at the root (root-first ordering per §4.2); partial rotation does not re-admit them.
